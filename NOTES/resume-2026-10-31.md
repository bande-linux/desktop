## Decisions waiting for the owner
- **Licence:** there is no LICENSE file and no crate has a licence field; this blocks Debian
  (`docs/debian-readiness.md`).
- **Naming:**
  - the clash with Debian's `bande` DNS resolver packages;
  - the `org.bande.*` domain.
- **Keyboard:**
  - Super+key → Ctrl+key in third-party apps, or Firefox's `accelKey` only: decided
    2026-11-01, neither. Ctrl is the app key in Bande's own apps too and Super the system key
    (`docs/decisions/0005-ctrl-for-app-shortcuts.md`, plan `NOTES/plans/super-ctrl.md`);
  - tap Super to open the launcher, `Alt+Tab`, and Super+arrows tiling (milestone 10 of that
    plan).
- **Product:** a welcome assistant; the audio player (Totem or Rhythmbox); which languages come first.
- **Files:** tags, Connect to Server.
- **Panels and the bar:**
  - clicking the clock while Control Center is open: the newest panel takes the keyboard, or the
    shell closes Control Center;
  - a bar click while the launcher is open only closes the launcher.
- **Text size:** title-bar toolbars stay at 100 % at large sizes.
- **Protocols:** keep or remove the unused private `set_corner_radius` request.
- **Debian policy:**
  - make the greeter opt-in;
  - the power key through an inhibitor instead of the logind drop-in;
  - the password-free PackageKit polkit rule.
- **Shortcuts:** the keys chosen here (Super+F11, Alt+Super+N, Alt+Super+C, Alt+Shift+Super+C for
  Copy as Pathname).
