# Camera

Domain: [camera](../../architecture/intents/domains/camera.md). Package A6. Size M.

**There is.** PipeWire and GStreamer in the screenshot tool, the Access portal. No camera code, and
no camera on the development computer.

**Work.** Cameras through PipeWire and libcamera; preview, pictures, video with sound, timer,
mirrored preview; a code reader on request; the indicator while in use; Settings' camera
permissions.

**Done when** it works on real cameras of two kinds, which the plan waits for.
