# Taskbar logo still shows a "U" (bande-desktop 0.1.1)

## Cause
- 698db5f8 is in the release: it is an ancestor of b339cb19 (and of v0.1.0). The shipped
  `/usr/bin/bande-shell` from the apt pool (bande-shell_0.1.1_arm64.deb) contains `mask id="b"`
  and no `mask id="u"`.
- But 698db5f8 changed only `Symbol::Logo` in `crates/shell/bar/src/symbols.rs:56`, which only the
  **separate** menu bar draws (`menu_bar.rs:395`, `Slot::BandeMenu => Symbol::Logo`).
- The **combined** taskbar, the default layout (`services/config/src/lib.rs:121-123`,
  `#[default] Combined`), is drawn by `crates/shell/bar/src/rail.rs` on every edge (vertical and
  horizontal). Its mark is not `Symbol::Logo`: `rail.rs:50` `MARK_GLYPH = "bande-symbolic"`,
  loaded from the icon theme at `rail.rs:505-519` (`find_icon` + `Image::load_symbolic`, 22 pt).
- That icon, `data/icons/Bande/symbolic/status/bande-symbolic.svg`, is generated by
  `data/icons/src/symbols.py:590` `bande_mark()`: an arc plus two uprights plus a dot, i.e. the
  old Unbound "U". The shipped .deb's copy is byte-identical to the repo's.
- Not a regression: the rail has used the theme glyph since it was created (a1b569e4,
  `"unbound-symbolic"`), renamed to `bande-symbolic` in f5b28aa7. 9f981602 (bar layouts) and the
  menu-button work did not touch it; nothing restored an old path. No greeter, lock screen or
  other consumer draws the mark (only `rail.rs` references `bande-symbolic`).

## Fix
1. `rail.rs` Slot::BandeMenu: draw the mark from the one built-in source,
   `symbols::svg(Symbol::Logo, ink)` rasterised by `Image::from_svg` at `MARK_SIZE * scale`,
   cached like the other rail images (key by `(Symbol::Logo, ink, pixels)` or reuse a `Symbols`
   with a size argument). Remove `MARK_GLYPH`.
2. Delete `bande_mark` and its `'bande-symbolic'` entry in `data/icons/src/symbols.py`, and the
   generated `data/icons/Bande/symbolic/status/bande-symbolic.svg` (generate.py prunes stale SVGs
   on regeneration). One drawing of the mark, so the two layouts cannot drift again.
   macOS reference: the Apple mark is a fixed built-in glyph, not a themeable icon.
3. `docs/design/datum.md` already describes the B; no doc change beyond that.

## Risks
- The B mask path is designed in a 16 unit box with stroke 1.9; at 22 pt the knock-out stroke is
  about 2.6 pt, heavier than in the menu bar (15 pt). Check it against the rail's 22 pt glyphs;
  if too heavy, give the rail mark the menu bar's 15 pt size or scale the stroke by size.
- Theme overrides of `bande-symbolic` (none known) stop applying. Intended.

## Verification
- `desktop-check`, then owner look on the mini: combined bar left, bottom and top edges show the
  B; Settings > Desktop > Layout Separate still shows the same B; light and dark ink.
- No e2e test (pure drawing).

## Size
Small: about 15 lines in rail.rs, a 5-line deletion in symbols.py, one SVG removed. Not a
one-line fix, so planned only. Ships to apt users only with the next release tag.

## Owner decision
- Mark size in the combined bar: keep 22 pt (recommended, it is the bar's identity glyph) or match
  the menu bar's 15 pt.
