# Recorder

Domain: [recordings](../../architecture/intents/domains/recordings.md). Package A6. Size M.

**There is.** `bande-audio`, the media engine, recording in the screenshot tool.

**Work.** Record from a chosen microphone to Opus or FLAC, pause, mark, trim, name, share; a list;
the microphone indicator; recording goes on with the window closed and says so; noise suppression as
a choice; export through the domain.

**Done when** a two-hour recording survives a crash up to its last second, and nothing records
without the indicator.
